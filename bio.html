Hello, I'm Mohit

I'm a Wireless and Automotive Engineer who builds technology at the intersection of connectivity and mobility. I currently work in Qualcomm R&D, within the Automotive Connected Car group, where my work spans 5G/6G wireless systems, RF engineering, and automotive applications — turning cutting-edge research into solutions that work in the real world.

Over the years I've focused on wireless communications and automotive systems, work that has led to multiple patents and publications in leading engineering journals. Alongside my engineering career, I'm the cofounder and co-owner of TimeFunction, a tool that automatically builds chronological timelines from Wikipedia and other web pages.

Outside of engineering, my wife and I are building Heal by Chocolate, a local craft chocolate brand that we currently make and sell. I'm also an active angel investor through two San Diego–based funds — NuFund Venture Group and Rising Tide Partners — and I spend my free time on digital illustration and photography.

Disclosure: I work at Qualcomm, but everything shared here and on my personal websites and social media accounts reflects my own views. My side projects, ventures, investments, and opinions are personal and do not represent Qualcomm or reflect its positions in any way.
